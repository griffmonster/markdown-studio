Authoring Guide & Pipeline Rules

1. Top-Level Section Rules

Only specific top-level headings (`#`) are permitted by the XSLT pipeline schema. Unrecognized top-level headings will fail validation:

  • # Description
  • # Directions
  • # Notes (Supports ## References)
  • # Pubs (Supports ## Pub Name, Place name)
  • # Features (Supports ## Feature Name)
  • # Updates
  • # Images
  • # Validation (Supports ## Date sub-headers)

2. Auto-Backup & Recovery

Your work is automatically saved to your browser's local storage on every keystroke. If you accidentally close the tab, your draft will be automatically restored when you reopen the page.

3. Pre-Flight Validation

Click Run XSLT Pre-Check before exporting to verify that all your section headings adhere to the whitelist and that no duplicate header IDs exist.
